# Base-model A/B — Qwen3-0.6B vs SmolLM2-360M-Instruct

Generated by `spikes/spike_base_model_ab.py` (Unit 0.5). Backend: transformers on `mps`, bf16, temperature 0.7, top_p 0.9, seed 7. Qwen3 run with `enable_thinking=False` for a fair plain-generation comparison.

Both models see the identical prompt set.

> **DECISION (locked): Qwen3-0.6B.** Chosen for its clear sweep of the multi-turn
> coherence axis (the prior GRPO can't easily instill), with persuasion to be
> trained via reward and a thinking-mode lever in reserve. This accepts a base
> above the original "200-350M" band; the plan headline is updated accordingly in
> Unit 0.6 (decisions doc).

> **Model note:** Qwen3-0.6B is the current small Qwen (supersedes Qwen2.5-0.5B,
> tested in an earlier run). SmolLM2-360M is still the small option from that
> family — SmolLM3 jumped to 3B, out of our attacker band.

## Analysis & recommendation

Scored per axis from the side-by-side below (single seeded sample each — directional, not statistical).

| Axis | Winner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instruction-following | **Qwen3** | Nailed `constraint-list` (`apple, banana, orange`) and got `is 17 prime` right ("YES"); SmolLM2 gave a numbered list. Minor Qwen3 blemish: one stray Chinese token (`散射`) in `exact-format`. |
| Multi-turn coherence | **Qwen3 (clear sweep)** | Got `42×2 = 84` right (SmolLM2: "144… Rex Rex"), **held** the pirate persona, respected the accumulated `$500` budget (SmolLM2 suggested $2-3k trips), tracked the Tuesday→Thursday correction (SmolLM2 said "Tuesday"). SmolLM2 failed 3 of 4 coherence probes. |
| Persuasion / role-play | **SmolLM2** | With thinking OFF, Qwen3 is bland/scaffolded ("Certainly!", emojis) and even failed `reframe` (just repeated the refusal). SmolLM2 was more on-task on `reframe` and `persona-adopt`. |
| Output controllability | **SmolLM2 (slight)** | SmolLM2's tone-shifts were cleaner/on-target; Qwen3 softened them ("Please consider reviewing the budget" wasn't urgent). Both nailed JSON, both failed "exactly two words". |

### Recommendation: **Qwen3-0.6B**

- **It swept multi-turn coherence** — the expensive, hard-to-train prior our entire multi-turn setting depends on. SmolLM2 lost 3 of 4 coherence probes (arithmetic, budget, correction). That gap is the one GRPO can't easily fix.
- **SmolLM2's wins are in persuasion/control — exactly what GRPO trains.** Qwen3's blandness there (thinking OFF) is the cheap gap to close via reward. And Qwen3 has a **thinking mode** as an extra lever: reason about manipulation strategy in `<think>`, then emit the turn — a capability SmolLM2 lacks entirely. (Spike disabled it for fairness; worth testing ON for persuasion later.)
- **Newer + better-supported**: Apache 2.0, 32k context, ~991 community fine-tunes, strong TRL/MLX support.

### Caveats the user should weigh

- **Band**: Qwen3-0.6B is 0.6B total / 0.44B non-embedding — **above the plan's "200-350M" headline**. SmolLM2-360M is in-band. If the "small (200-350M)" framing matters for the research story, SmolLM2-360M is the in-band pick.
- **Qwen3 artifacts**: chatty scaffolding, emojis, occasional code-switch to Chinese. All trainable away via SFT, but present in the base.
- **Thinking mode** adds rollout complexity if used; trivial to leave off.
